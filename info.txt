This prototype introduces new work in the forward and return paths like tunneling, encryption,
encapsulation, etc. This therefore slightly increases overhead and decreases throughput:

The tunnel introduces processing overhead for high-throughput TCP transfers 
but still provides stable transport for UDP at the tested rates from 500K 
upto 50M (with negligible loss of 0.02%). At 100M throughput decreased to 
88.2 with 1.8% indicating that it had reached its processing capacity

//verdict
While the tunnel has significantly lower throughput for TCP/UDP (with a 100M capacity),
unlike its direct counterpart; it prioritizes session continuity and seamless migration,
so some throughput overhead is an acceptable trade-off, provided the tunnel remains stable 
and reliable. 

In addition the current overhead is expected in a prototype implementation and could be 
optimized in the future.

Improvements for efficiency:
- Increase the TUN MTU (e.g. to 1400 instead of 1000).
- Remove or reduce printf() calls inside packet-processing paths.
- Minimize unnecessary packet copies.
- Avoid mutex contention where possible.
